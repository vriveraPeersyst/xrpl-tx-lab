## What changes

When a [Payment](/tx/Payment) changes currency it uses the order book, the same way [OfferCreate](/tx/OfferCreate) does when crossing offers. However, both paths consumed liquidity slightly differently: the payment engine could take more "quality" from an offer than it was entitled to, or leave offer remnants that offer crossing would have removed. The consequence was that two economically equivalent operations produced different results, and some offers ended up in the book in an inconsistent state.

fix1515 aligns the payment engine's offer consumption with offer crossing's: the same rule decides how much is taken from each offer and when it's considered exhausted.

## Affected transactions and objects

- [Payment](/tx/Payment) between different currencies (with or without `Paths`).
- [OfferCreate](/tx/OfferCreate): behavioral reference.
- [Offer](/objects/Offer): how it's reduced or removed when consumed.

## Status and context

Numbered after rippled issue #1515. It was introduced in version 1.7.0 and is retired in the current code (`XRPL_RETIRE_FIX(1515)`). It's part of a series of DEX fixes (along with [fixTakerDryOfferRemoval](/amendments/fixTakerDryOfferRemoval) and [fixRmSmallIncreasedQOffers](/amendments/fixRmSmallIncreasedQOffers)) aimed at ensuring the order book never contains offers that can't be executed.
