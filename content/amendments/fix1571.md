## What changes

Fixes two problems with [Escrow](/objects/Escrow):

1. **Escrows without a condition or a release time.** Previously an escrow could be created with only `CancelAfter`, without `Condition` or `FinishAfter`. That escrow could be finished immediately by anyone, which made it useless as a holding mechanism. With fix1571, [EscrowCreate](/tx/EscrowCreate) requires in `preflight` that at least one of `Condition` or `FinishAfter` be present; otherwise it returns `temMALFORMED`.

2. **Time check in EscrowFinish.** [EscrowFinish](/tx/EscrowFinish) compared `FinishAfter` against the current ledger's close time instead of the parent ledger's (`parentCloseTime`), which is the canonical reference used by the rest of the protocol's deadlines. That allowed finishing an escrow one ledger earlier than it should, or, depending on the case, delaying it. fix1571 uses `parentCloseTime` consistently in `EscrowFinish` and [EscrowCancel](/tx/EscrowCancel).

## Affected transactions and objects

- [EscrowCreate](/tx/EscrowCreate): new validation rule.
- [EscrowFinish](/tx/EscrowFinish) and [EscrowCancel](/tx/EscrowCancel): time reference.
- [Escrow](/objects/Escrow).

## Status and context

Numbered after rippled issue #1571. It was introduced in version 1.2.0 and is retired (`XRPL_RETIRE_FIX(1571)`). If on testnet you try to create an escrow with only `CancelAfter`, the `temMALFORMED` you get comes from this rule.
